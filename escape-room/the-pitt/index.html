You have penetrated the outer perimeter of STRYKER DEEP SECTOR 7 — once the most advanced medical research installation on the continent. What you find here may save what remains of civilization. Or bury you with it.

YEAR: 2147. The Collapse came fast and hard. Nineteen months of cascading grid failures, supply chain collapse, and a pathogen that turned surgeries into gambles. The old world's medical infrastructure crumbled in under a year.

But the legends survived. Survivors speak of a pre-Collapse corporation — STRYKER — that had been quietly engineering a new era of medicine. Robotics. Precision instruments. Solutions humanity had never seen. When the Collapse hit, their primary R&D bunker — codenamed THE PITT — sealed itself and went dark.

You are here because three clues led you to this door. The bunker AI, ARIS (Autonomous Research Intelligence System), is still active. It will not open the vaults for just anyone. You must prove you understand what STRYKER built — and why they built it.
