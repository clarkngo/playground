How to use this supplement
📖
Read first
Skim the chapter to get the vocabulary. Come here when a concept feels abstract.
🎛️
Break governance
Toggle controls off, remove stewards, skip approvals. See what fails and why.
🧩
Play each scenario
Each chapter has 2-3 realistic scenarios. The differences between them reveal what governance is protecting against.
📋
Map to your org
Ask yourself which maturity level or RACI pattern matches your current team. That gap is where to start.
