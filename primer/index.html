ℹ️ About Industry Primer

Industry Primer is a growing knowledge base that builds foundational understanding of different industries — their terminology, key metrics, major players, common workflows, and how technology intersects with each sector.

Each industry primer is structured into subsections covering:

  • Overview — what the industry does and why it matters
  • Key Terminology — essential vocabulary and jargon
  • Major Players — companies, roles, and stakeholders
  • Core Metrics — how success is measured
  • Technology Stack — tools, platforms, and systems used
  • Common Workflows — day-to-day processes and pipelines
  • Trends & Challenges — where the industry is heading

New industries are added progressively via scheduled prompts.
