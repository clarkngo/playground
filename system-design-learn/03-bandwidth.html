Bandwidth & Data Flow

Bandwidth is the width of your data pipe — how many bits can flow per second. A 1 Gbps network link sounds fast until you have 10,000 concurrent users each streaming 4K video. Understanding ingress (data coming in) vs egress (data going out) is critical because cloud providers charge for egress — sometimes more than the compute itself.

💸 Unit Conversion Cheat Sheet
KB/s → Mbps: KB/s × 0.008
MB/s → Gbps: MB/s × 0.008
GB/s → Tbps: GB/s × 0.008
1 Gbps = 125 MB/s
1 TB/day = 11.6 MB/s = 92.6 Mbps
1 PB/day = 11.6 GB/s = 92.6 Gbps
