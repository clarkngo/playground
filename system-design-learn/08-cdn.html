CDN & Origin Server Offload

A CDN (Content Delivery Network) caches your content at edge nodes close to users worldwide. Instead of every user fetching assets from your origin server in one datacenter, they get them from a PoP (Point of Presence) a few milliseconds away. The benefits are dual: lower latency for users, and dramatically lower costs for you.
