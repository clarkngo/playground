The Formula
Avg RPS = (DAU × req/day) ÷ 86,400

Peak RPS = Avg RPS × multiplier

86,400 = seconds in a day. Always divide by this to go from daily to per-second.

📐
The Peak Multiplier

Traffic is never uniform. A 3× multiplier means your busiest hour has 3× the average load. Most systems see 2–5× peaks. Twitter's Super Bowl spike was reported at ~6×.

⚖️
Reads vs Writes

Most systems are heavily read-skewed. Twitter is ~100:1 reads to writes. Instagram is similar. This shapes architecture — read replicas for reads, single primary for writes.
