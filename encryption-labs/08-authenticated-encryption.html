❓ Which mode detects the tampering and refuses to decrypt?

💡 Why it matters

The silent acceptance of tampered ciphertext in CBC is exploited by Padding Oracle attacks (POODLE, Lucky13) and CBC bit-flip attacks. GCM's authentication tag closes this gap entirely. The rule: if you need both confidentiality and integrity (which is almost always), use an AEAD cipher — AES-256-GCM or ChaCha20-Poly1305.
