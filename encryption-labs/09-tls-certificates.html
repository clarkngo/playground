💡 Why it matters

Certificate verification is what makes HTTPS trustworthy. When it fails — expired cert, wrong domain, untrusted CA — browsers show a red warning. Without it, BGP hijacking or DNS spoofing lets attackers intercept your traffic with their own key. This is why certificate pinning, CAA DNS records, and Certificate Transparency logs matter for high-value targets.
