❓ What is the minimum key size NIST recommends for modern symmetric encryption (AES)?

💡 Why it matters

Key length is the fundamental security parameter. Every extra bit doubles the key space. Going from 128-bit to 256-bit doesn't just double the work — it raises it to the power of 2128. AES-256 also provides a quantum-resistance margin: Grover's algorithm would reduce it to ~128-bit equivalent security, still far beyond reach.
