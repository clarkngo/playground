💡 Why it matters

SHA-256 produces a unique 256-bit "fingerprint" of any data. Even changing a single character produces a completely different hash — this is the avalanche effect. This underpins code signing (software updates), blockchain integrity, TLS certificate verification, and file download verification. Without integrity checks, a man-in-the-middle can silently modify documents or software in transit.
