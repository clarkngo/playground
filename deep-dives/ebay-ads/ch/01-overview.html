Platform Overview

The eBay Ads Platform is a sophisticated, distributed advertising infrastructure that powers one of the largest programmatic advertising ecosystems globally. It handles billions of events daily—clicks, impressions, conversions—with real-time processing requirements and 99.99% uptime SLA.

Core Pillars

🎯 Ad Programs

Multiple product tiers: PLS (Cost-Per-Sale), PLA (CPC with keyword bidding), PLX (Simplified), SFA (Programmatic), Promoted Display.

📊 Real-Time Processing

Kafka streams billions of events into Flink and Spark pipelines for instant metrics, aggregations, and anomaly detection.

🔧 Infrastructure

Kubernetes-based microservices with containerized deployments, multi-region failover, and automated scaling.

⚙️ Operations

24/7 SRE team using Control Center, Sherlock, Prometheus/Grafana for monitoring and incident response.

💰 Monetization

Multiple revenue models: CPM, CPC, CPA/CPO, Fixed-price. CRM integration with Hive (Salesforce).

🧪 Quality Assurance

Continuous A/B testing via Touchstone, anomaly detection, fraud prevention, and automated SLA monitoring.

Scale & Complexity

  • Billions of events daily — clicks, impressions, conversions tracked in real-time
  • Petabytes of historical data — stored in Hadoop (Apollo/Hermes) for analytics
  • Multi-region deployment — distributed across global data centers with sub-millisecond latency
  • 99.99% uptime SLA — critical infrastructure for revenue generation
  • Sub-100ms ad serving — required for real-time bidding auctions
