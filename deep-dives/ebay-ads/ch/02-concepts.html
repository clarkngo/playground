Advertising Concepts & Business Models

Advertising Programs

Program Pricing Model Use Case
PLS
Promoted Listings Standard		 Cost-Per-Sale (CPS) Beginner sellers. Pay percentage of sale price after item sells. Simplest model.
PLA
Promoted Listings Advanced		 Cost-Per-Click (CPC) Advanced sellers. Keyword bidding with real-time auctions. ML-powered bid recommendations.
PLX
Promoted Listings Express		 Simplified CPC Auto-bidding with less configuration. Bridge between PLS and PLA.
SFA
Seller-Funded Ads		 Auction/CPM eBay-owned inventory (off-platform, affiliate). Sold via DSP/SSP programmatic platform.
Promoted Display CPM / Fixed Price Banner ads and brand solutions. Guaranteed/non-guaranteed placements. Premium campaigns.

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

GMV (Gross Merchandise Value)

Total sales amount driven through the platform. Primary business metric.

PL-Enabled GMV

GMV directly attributable to Promoted Listings. Measures ad impact on revenue.

ROAS (Return on Ad Spend)

GMV ÷ Ad Revenue. Measures campaign efficiency and profitability.

Attribution Ratio

Percentage of sales attributed to ad clicks. Tracks true ad contribution.

Take Rate / Sold Ad Rate

Effective fee rate charged for sold ads. Revenue per transaction.

CTR & Conversion Rate

Clicks ÷ Impressions. Sales ÷ Clicks. Funnel metrics for optimization.

Ad Tech Ecosystem

DSP (Demand-Side Platform) — eBay-owned tool for programmatic ad buying. Allows sellers and advertisers to purchase inventory in real-time auctions.
SSP (Supply-Side Platform) — eBay-owned tool for inventory management. Publishers and sellers use SSP to monetize impressions.
RTB (Real-Time Bidding) — Instantaneous auction environment where impressions are bought/sold programmatically. Sub-100ms latency requirement.
