💡 Why it matters

PCI DSS (Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard) explicitly prohibits verify=False for any connection handling cardholder data. In 2015 researchers demonstrated a MITM attack against iOS apps that used NSURLSession with validation disabled, intercepting payment data from popular apps within minutes on a coffee shop network. Even in internal Kubernetes/Docker networks, MITM is possible via ARP spoofing or a compromised sidecar container. Fix: never disable certificate verification. If using self-signed certs internally, pin the CA certificate: verify='/path/to/internal-ca.pem'.
