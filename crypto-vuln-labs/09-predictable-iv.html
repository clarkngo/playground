💡 Why it matters

The BEAST attack (2011) against TLS 1.0 exploited predictable CBC IVs to decrypt HTTPS cookies in under 30 minutes. The attack works because in CBC, the IV is XORed with the plaintext before encryption — a known IV lets an attacker craft a specific plaintext that, when encrypted, reveals information about a target block. Timestamps repeat across server restarts and are observable from log metadata. Fix: always generate IVs with os.urandom(16) immediately before each encryption call — never derive them from time, counters, or any deterministic source.
