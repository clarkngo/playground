💡 Why it matters

The "Penguin Problem" is the canonical ECB demonstration: encrypt a bitmap image with AES-ECB and the penguin silhouette remains perfectly visible in the ciphertext because identical 16-byte pixel blocks produce identical ciphertext blocks. For medical images, this can reveal anatomical structures directly from encrypted data. Fix: always use an authenticated mode — modes.GCM(iv) with a fresh random IV per encryption provides both confidentiality and integrity in one step.
