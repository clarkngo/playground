💡 Why it matters

In 2012 the RockYou breach exposed 32 million passwords stored in plaintext. When LinkedIn was breached that same year, 117 million MD5-hashed passwords were cracked in days — no salt meant identical passwords produced identical hashes, and GPU rigs test 100 billion MD5 hashes per second. Fix: use bcrypt, argon2-cffi, or hashlib.scrypt(). These are designed to be slow. A bcrypt hash with cost factor 12 takes ~250ms to compute — making brute-force infeasible even after a breach.
