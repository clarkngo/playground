💡 Why it matters

In 2012 Dropbox was breached and 68 million bcrypt-hashed passwords were stolen. Despite being a large breach, the slow hash made cracking infeasible for most accounts. Contrast with the 2016 LinkedIn breach: 117 million passwords hashed with unsalted SHA-1 were cracked in days. A raw-password-as-key scheme is even worse than SHA-1 hashing because it applies zero computational cost. Fix: use hashlib.scrypt(password, salt=os.urandom(16), n=2**14, r=8, p=1, dklen=32) or the cryptography library's Scrypt KDF. Store the random salt alongside the ciphertext.
