📋 Requirements Engineering

Conversation to Design

Read a realistic team conversation, extract requirements, build a design doc section by section, and sequence the architecture — 10 interactive activities across real software domains.

How It Works
Step 1
Read the Conversation
Study a realistic Slack, email, or team thread. This is your only source of requirements — no spec sheet exists.
Step 2–5
Extract Requirements
Identify actors and goals, classify functional and non-functional requirements, and surface open questions.
Step 6
Build the Design Doc
Fill in the key sections of a real design document: problem statement, goals, non-goals, and tech stack.
Step 7
Architect the System
Sequence components in the correct data-flow order and match each to its responsibility in the system.

How to Use These Activities

Work through each activity in order for a progressive challenge. Each activity gives you a different domain, conversation format, and technical architecture to reason about. The 7-step workflow is the same across all activities — once you learn the pattern, focus shifts entirely to the domain-specific reasoning.
