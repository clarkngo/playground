Bandwidth & Data Volume

🎯 Scenario

You are provisioning network infrastructure for a multiplayer online gaming platform. Unlike web pages, games stream continuous real-time state updates in both directions. You need to calculate the total network bandwidth your data center must sustain at peak, and estimate your monthly data bill.

📋 Given Values

Peak concurrent players: 200,000
Data sent per player (upload): 50 KB/s
Data sent to player (download): 150 KB/s
Average daily active hours: 6 hours/day
Total daily active players: 1,000,000
Egress cost: $0.05 / GB
💡 Unit reference: 1 GB/s = 8 Gbps  |  1 TB/s = 1,000 GB/s  |  Bandwidth (bps) = throughput (B/s) × 8  |  1 GB = 1,024 MB ≈ 1,000 MB for estimates

🧮 Step-by-Step Calculations

Step 1 — Peak ingress bandwidth (player → server) in GB/s
= Peak players × 50 KB/s ÷ 1,000,000 (KB per GB)
GB/s
Step 2 — Peak egress bandwidth (server → player) in GB/s
= Peak players × 150 KB/s ÷ 1,000,000
GB/s
Step 3 — Peak egress in Gbps (for network provisioning)
= Peak egress GB/s × 8 (bits per byte)
Gbps
Step 4 — Daily egress data per player (in GB)
= 150 KB/s × 6 hours × 3,600 seconds ÷ 1,000,000
GB/player/day
Step 5 — Total daily egress (all players, in TB)
= GB per player × 1,000,000 daily players ÷ 1,000
TB/day
Step 6 — Monthly egress volume (in TB)
= Daily TB × 30 days
TB/month
⭐ Step 7 — Monthly egress cost (USD)
= Monthly TB × 1,000 GB/TB × $0.05/GB
$
✅ Worked Solution
Step 1: 200,000 × 50 KB/s ÷ 1,000,000 = 10 GB/s ingress
Step 2: 200,000 × 150 KB/s ÷ 1,000,000 = 30 GB/s egress
Step 3: 30 GB/s × 8 = 240 Gbps (need 240G of uplink capacity)
Step 4: 150 KB/s × 6h × 3,600s ÷ 1,000,000 = 3.24 GB/player/day
Step 5: 3.24 GB × 1,000,000 ÷ 1,000 = 3,240 TB/day
Step 6: 3,240 × 30 = 97,200 TB/month
Step 7: 97,200 × 1,000 × $0.05 = $4,860,000/month
📌 Engineer's takeaway: Nearly $5M/month in egress costs is why game companies invest heavily in peer-to-peer (P2P) networking, private network transit deals, and regional server co-location to avoid cloud egress fees. AWS, GCP, and Azure all charge significantly for outbound data — this calculation justifies owning data center infrastructure at this scale.
