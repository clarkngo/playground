Storage Capacity Estimation

🎯 Scenario

You are an infrastructure engineer at an Instagram-scale photo sharing platform. Before signing a storage contract with a cloud provider, you need to estimate how much raw storage you will need — today and for the next 5 years. Getting this wrong means either paying for unused capacity or running out of space unexpectedly.

📋 Given Values

Daily Active Users (DAU): 500,000,000
% of users who post per day: 10%
Average photo size: 2 MB
Text metadata per post: 500 bytes
Replication factor: 3× copies
Retention period: 5 years
💡 Unit reference: 1 MB = 1,048,576 bytes ≈ 106 bytes (use 106 for estimates)  |  1 TB = 106 MB  |  1 PB = 106 GB = 109 MB

🧮 Step-by-Step Calculations

Step 1 — Daily posts created
= DAU × 10% = 500,000,000 × 0.10
posts/day
Step 2 — Daily photo storage (in MB)
= Daily posts × 2 MB per photo
MB/day
Step 3 — Daily photo storage (convert to TB)
= Daily photo MB ÷ 1,000,000 (MB per TB)
TB/day
Step 4 — Daily metadata storage (in GB)
= Daily posts × 500 bytes ÷ 1,000,000,000
GB/day
Step 5 — Raw storage needed per year (in TB)
≈ Daily photo TB × 365 (metadata is negligible at this scale)
TB/year
Step 6 — Raw storage over 5 years (in PB)
= Annual TB × 5 years ÷ 1,000 (TB per PB)
PB (raw)
⭐ Step 7 — Total storage with 3× replication (in PB)
= Raw 5-year PB × replication factor (3)
PB total
✅ Worked Solution
Step 1: 500,000,000 × 0.10 = 50,000,000 posts/day
Step 2: 50,000,000 × 2 MB = 100,000,000 MB/day
Step 3: 100,000,000 ÷ 1,000,000 = 100 TB/day
Step 4: 50,000,000 × 500 ÷ 1,000,000,000 = 25 GB/day (≈ 0.025 TB — negligible)
Step 5: 100 TB × 365 = 36,500 TB/year
Step 6: 36,500 TB × 5 ÷ 1,000 = 182.5 PB raw
Step 7: 182.5 × 3 = 547.5 PB total (with 3× replication)
📌 Engineer's takeaway: ~547 PB over 5 years is in the same order of magnitude as Instagram's real storage footprint. This is why cloud providers offer tiered storage — hot data in fast SSD, cold data in cheaper object storage (e.g., AWS S3 Glacier). Engineers also apply deduplication and compression to reduce this by 30–60%.
