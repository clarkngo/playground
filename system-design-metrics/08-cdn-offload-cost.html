CDN Offload & Cost Savings

📋 Scenario

A news website serves 50 million page views per day. Each page view transfers an average of 800 KB of assets (images, JS, CSS). Without a CDN, all traffic hits the origin servers. The team introduces a CDN with a 78% cache hit rate. Origin egress costs $0.09 per GB; CDN egress costs $0.02 per GB. Calculate the daily data volume, origin load with and without CDN, and the monthly cost comparison.

Daily Page Views
50M
Assets per View
800 KB
CDN Hit Rate
78%
Origin Egress
$0.09/GB
CDN Egress
$0.02/GB
Days / Month
30
Unit Reference: 1 GB = 1,024 MB = 1,048,576 KB | Daily GB = (page views × KB per view) / 1,048,576 | Origin GB w/ CDN = Daily GB × (1 − hit rate) | Monthly cost = daily GB × 30 × price/GB

🧮 Step-by-Step Calculation

Step 1 — Total daily data volume (GB)
Formula: (50,000,000 views × 800 KB) / 1,048,576 KB per GB
GB/day
Step 2 — Origin load per day WITHOUT CDN (GB)
Formula: Same as Step 1 (all traffic hits origin)
GB/day
Step 3 — Origin load per day WITH CDN (GB) — cache misses only
Formula: Total GB/day × (1 − 0.78)
GB/day
Step 4 — Monthly origin cost WITHOUT CDN ($)
Formula: Daily GB × 30 days × $0.09/GB
$/month
Step 5 — Monthly CDN egress cost WITH CDN ($)
Formula: Total GB/day × 30 × $0.02/GB (CDN charges for all traffic served)
$/month
Step 6 — Monthly origin egress cost WITH CDN ($)
Formula: Origin GB/day × 30 × $0.09/GB
$/month
Step 7 — Monthly savings by using a CDN ($)
Formula: No-CDN cost − (CDN egress cost + origin egress cost)
$/month saved

✅ Worked Solution

Total daily GB = (50M × 800KB) / 1,048,576 ≈ 38,147 GB/day
Origin w/o CDN = 38,147 GB/day (all hits origin)
Origin w/ CDN = 38,147 × 0.22 ≈ 8,392 GB/day
No-CDN cost = 38,147 × 30 × $0.09 ≈ $102,997/month
CDN egress cost = 38,147 × 30 × $0.02 ≈ $22,888/month
Origin egress = 8,392 × 30 × $0.09 ≈ $22,659/month
Monthly savings = $102,997 − ($22,888 + $22,659) ≈ $57,450/month
Engineer's Takeaway: A 78% CDN hit rate reduces origin load by ~78% and total egress cost by ~55%. CDN egress is ~4.5× cheaper than origin egress, so the savings compound fast. Beyond cost, a CDN also reduces latency (edge PoPs closer to users) and protects the origin from traffic spikes — making it both a cost and reliability decision.
