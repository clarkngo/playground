Social Media News Feed (Fanout)

📰 The Objective

A user posts a photo on a platform with 500 million active users. Within seconds, all of their followers should see that post at the top of their feed — without the feed load being slow.

Think about: What service stores the post and triggers the distribution process? How does a single post get pushed into every follower's personal feed? Where should each follower's feed be pre-stored so it loads instantly when they open the app? What is the final step that delivers the pre-built feed to the client?
